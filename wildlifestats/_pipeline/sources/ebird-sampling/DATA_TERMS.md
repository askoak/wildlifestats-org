# eBird Sampling Data Terms

Source: Cornell Lab of Ornithology eBird sampling archive distributed inside
`ebd_sampling_relMay-2026.tar`.

This pilot is constrained by the source terms packaged with the archive:

- Non-commercial use only.
- Use is limited to research and education.
- The original dataset must not be passed to third parties.
- The original dataset must not be published or publicly redistributed in its
  original format, in whole or in part.
- Derived data may be shared only if the same terms travel with the derivative.
- Citation and acknowledgement are required for downstream use.

WildlifeStats pilot policy for this source:

- Keep the raw archive local on `Hello`.
- Do not extract raw rows into the git repo.
- Commit aggregate-only denominator outputs.
- Carry the terms forward in this file and the paired provenance/results
  records.

Recommended citation bundled with the archive:

> eBird Basic Dataset. Version: EBD_relMay-2026. Cornell Lab of Ornithology,
> Ithaca, New York. May 2026.
